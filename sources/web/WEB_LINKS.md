## Nautical Almanac / geocentric apparent place

- **Title:** Glossary, The Astronomical Almanac (U.S. Naval Observatory)
- **Quote (Geocentric):** "with reference to, or pertaining to, the center of the Earth."
- **Quote (Geocentric Coordinates):** "1. The latitude and longitude of a point on the Earth's surface relative to the center of the Earth. 2. Celestial coordinates given with respect to the center of the Earth."
- **Quote (Sub-earth Point):** "the point on a body's surface that lies directly beneath the Earth on the line connecting the body's center to the geocenter."

## Geographical position (Wikipedia — Celestial navigation)

- **Title:** Celestial navigation — Wikipedia
- **Quote:** "For navigation by celestial means, when on the surface of the Earth at any given instant in time, a celestial body is located directly over a single point on the Earth's surface. The latitude and longitude of that point are known as the celestial body's geographic position (GP)."
